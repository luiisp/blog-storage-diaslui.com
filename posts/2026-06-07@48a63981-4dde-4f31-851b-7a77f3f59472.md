## Us

[Humans (taxonomically Homo sapiens, a term derived from the Latin for "wise man", also known as person, people, or mankind) are the only surviving species of the genus Homo and the most abundant and widespread primates on Earth. We are characterized by bipedalism and a large brain, which allowed the development of advanced tools, cultures, and languages.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human)

We exist, and that is a fact. Human beings think, reason, execute, and plan.
Inside every human being lies a universe and millions of processes happening simultaneously just so that being can exist. 

Nothing is ever static; if it were to stop, it would mean the end of life, everything would become a void. And that is why life is beautiful, and it needs to be cherished and lived. But merely existing is not enough: just like the millions of chemical combinations that allow us to live, we must be in motion—to grow, to build, to do.

And throughout time, we did just that, and it was beautiful.

Mathematics, physics, chemistry, computing...

The world gained color, the skies were pierced, and people were able to truly live.

However, we built so much, so very much, that we ended up building what might pose a risk to our own extinction: AI.

---

## The AI Era

Before AI, no one else thought besides us, human beings. Yet, we created thinking machines and now, alongside us, there are machines that "think". So, would this mean the end of thought?

The risk of AI is not the technology itself, but how we look at it.

The simplest tasks, like writing a letter or even a text like this one, can now be done by AI, so the tendency is for people to ask themselves: "What for?"

Why study mathematics if AI can already solve these equations for me?
Why study computing if AI can already build things?

An existential void takes over when AI is placed as humanity's competitor. After all, we have never had competitors before, and we do not know how to handle one.

But is AI really our competitor?

We got here alone, we created all of this alone—just Humanity. We created AI, but what now?

---
## Surfing

Now is the time to create something greater. Time, our most scarce resource, no longer needs to be wasted on basic tasks; they can be handled by AI. At the same time, we cannot afford to forget how to do them ourselves (fundamentals matter).

It is time to use our minds and think—to think about bigger things.
The clever ones will seize this opportunity and surf this wave; therefore, greater things are yet to come.

This text is an invitation for you to use your mind, an invitation to build something greater and be a part of it all.

Do not let the movement stop.

**What is life without... movement?**
